# Indonesia Emas 2045 (RPJPN 2025-2045) — validated register (N2)

17 November 2026. Validates the candidate register in `claude/indonesia-2045-review-scope.md` §3 line by line, applies the confirmed decisions in `claude/handoff-indonesia-2045-2026-11-17.md` §3, and adds the items listed there in §6.1. Method: `claude/scenario-review-method.md` v0.2, early-horizon variant (proposal 21).

## 0. How this register is built

**Scoring base.** Draft-annex extractions (`Indonesia-Bappenas-RPJPN-2025-2045-Draft/part1-figures.json`, `part2-*`) with the four overrides from the enacted law (decision 1): vision wording "NKRI yang Bersatu, Berdaulat, Maju, dan Berkelanjutan"; IKLH 76.49 → 83.00 added to target 5; formal-job-creation share 35 → 80 added to Tabel 4.2.2 (IE4); Tabel 4.5.1 row 42a replaced by four sub-indices.

**Citation.** Every page reference below is to the enacted text: "UU No. 59 Tahun 2024, Lampiran, printed p. N". Pages were located in the law's OCR layer (`Indonesia-UU-59-2024-RPJPN-Enacted/uu59-2024-lampiran-text.md`; PDF page = printed page + 37). Where the law's page differs from the draft page used in the scope doc, the law's page is given. Figures that the OCR could not read (chart captions inside images) are cited to the page where the body text refers to them.

**Classes and status** per method v0.2: Claim / Goal / Commitment; Stated / Implied. "Depends on" lists register IDs. "What would challenge it" is written before evidence is gathered.

**ID scheme.** F1-F13 framing assumptions and 1.1-6.1 chapter claims keep the scope doc's IDs. New: **I-n** = row n of the 45-indicator tables (Tabel 4.1.1-4.5.1); **S-n** = row of the five headline targets (Gambar 3.4.1); **R-n** = Tabel 5.2.1 regional rows; **GC-n** = game changer n (Gambar 3.7.1); **D-n** = direction IEn; **O-n** = 2017 oration-deck items (superseded vision, paired per decision 2); **N3-n** = consistency-log entries.

**Two working rules** (handoff §3): 2025 "baselines" are projections and are replaced by 2025 outturns in the evidence pass wherever BPS, BI or Kemenkeu have published them, with both values shown; the RPJMN 2025-2029 (Perpres 12/2025) is the operative medium-term path and is recorded beside the vision's own phase-1 path.

**Tracks on the page** (decision 2): RPJPN = early-horizon variant (claims tested, change scan in full; goals and commitments listed "not yet reviewed"); 2017 deck = superseded vision, reduced review, paired on the page.
