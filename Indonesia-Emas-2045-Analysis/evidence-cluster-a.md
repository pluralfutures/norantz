**Reading for the page (facts, not verdicts).** On the vision's own range (6-7 %), the growth arithmetic is not yet out of reach but requires the economy to grow faster for nineteen years than it has in any decade since 1990. On the RPJMN's operative path, the 2029 figures (8 % growth, USD 8,000) now require three years at rates last seen before 1997 and an Atlas-dollar pace last seen in 2001-11. Every international institution in the source pack projects 5.0-5.7 % for 2026-28. The GNI-per-capita path is the tighter constraint: it depends on the exchange rate and the threshold drift as well as on real growth, and the last decade's Atlas pace (3.1 %) is a third of what any of the 2045 targets need.
