## 5. Limits log for this pass

- **Tax ratio 9.31 %** is a DDTC/Kontan computation from Kemenkeu revenue (perpajakan Rp 2,217.9 tn) and BPS nominal GDP (Rp 23,821.1 tn); Kemenkeu publishes no single official 2025 tax-ratio figure in the sources read. The RPJPN/RPJMN definition (rasio pajak) may include natural-resource non-tax revenue (broad definition ≈ 10.3 % on the same computation) — definition drift to record under I-31.
- **Regulation counts** from the BPK database are not on the law's basis; the comparison is qualitative.
- **Danantara** figures are management statements; audited consolidated accounts for 2025 were not available as of July 2026.
- **Private share of infrastructure (28 %)** is a ministerial statement (Kemenko Infrastruktur, Sept 2025) without a published method.
- **CPI** is a Tier-2 composite; it is used because the law adopts it as indicator 27b.
- **Not done:** Lampiran II of Perpres 12/2025; Kemenkeu LKPP 2025 (audited, due mid-2026) for final revenue and deficit; KLH carbon-tax status; the state revenue agency (BPN) — whether created.
