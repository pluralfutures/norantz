## 5. Access notes and gaps

- **World Bank API/data pages** return a WAF block from the workspace; the data page fetch returned an empty table. GNI per capita figures must be re-read at source by a human before publication (S-1).
- **BPS** press-release pages render partially through fetch; the figures above come from the release abstracts and from the BPS Q1-2026 news page, cross-checked against three or more press reports of the same release.
- **Perpres 12/2025 Lampiran II** (year-by-year matrices) still unread (handoff §6.5).
- **No Tier-1 source publishes a potential-growth number for Indonesia at present**: BI states the cycle is below potential without a figure; IMF gives an output gap and a medium-term growth path (≈ 5,2 %). Potential growth is therefore inferred, and rated as such.
- **Middle class 2025**: BPS has not (as far as found) published its own 2025 series; the Mandiri Institute figure is Tier 2.
- **OECD Survey** is two years old; the next is expected with Indonesia's accession process.
- **Change-scan candidate noted in passing**: a Middle East conflict ("Iran war") in 2026 raised oil prices and prompted World Bank/OECD downgrades in April 2026 and BI rate rises in H1 2026 (press reports). Not evidenced here; belongs to X10/F11 in N6.
