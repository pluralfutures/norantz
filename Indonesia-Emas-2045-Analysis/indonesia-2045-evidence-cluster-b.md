## 2. Required-rate test — manufacturing share (the plan's chosen engine)

| Path | (1) Needed from 2025 base | (2) Achieved | (3) Now needed from 19.07 % (2025) | (4) Best on record | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1: 20.8 → 21.9 % by 2029 | +0.28 pt/yr | +0.24 pt/yr (2022-25); 2025 came in 1.7 pt below the base | **+0.71 pt/yr for 2026-29** | +0.24 pt/yr (2022-25) is the only sustained rise since 2005 (law p. 17: 27.41 % in 2005 → 18.34 % in 2022) | Needs three times the best recent pace. |
| Peak 30.0 % by 2039 | +0.66 pt/yr | as above | **+0.78 pt/yr for 14 years** | as above | — |
| 2045: 28.0 % | +0.36 pt/yr | as above | **+0.45 pt/yr for 20 years** | as above | Needs roughly twice the 2022-25 pace sustained for two decades; a 20-year rise of 9 points has no precedent in the series. |

**Reading for the page.** Manufacturing's share has stopped falling and has grown faster than the economy for two years; the direction the plan needs is present. The pace is not: the phase-1 end-point (21.9 % in 2029) now needs the share to rise three times faster than it has since 2022, and the 2045 figure needs a twenty-year rise without precedent in the post-2005 record. Exports as a share of GDP are above the plan's 2025 projection, driven by palm oil, metals and nickel products into China.

## 5. Limits log for this pass

- **BPS quarterly manufacturing shares for 2026** (19.20 / 18.50 / Q3) come from Kemenperin and press reports of the BPS releases; the annual 2023-25 shares (18.67 / 18.98 / 19.07) are BPS figures as reported in the 5 Feb 2026 press conference coverage. The BRS PDFs were not opened. The Q3-2026 share was truncated in the only report found; it is given as "in the 19 % range" and not used in any rate.
- **Series break (N3-10):** Kemenperin states that the 2010 KBLI revision moved three sub-sectors out of manufacturing, so the law's 27.4 % (2005) and later values are not on one basis. If so, part of the "premature deindustrialisation" fall is reclassification. Not verified against BPS methodology notes; log for the consistency check and ask BPS's own documentation before the page states the 2005-22 decline as a single series.
- **White House / USTR texts** on the ART were read through KPMG, Antara and law-firm summaries, not the fact sheet itself; terms are consistent across five carriers. The US court decisions on IEEPA tariffs are reported by one law-firm note (DFDL) and were not verified.
- **Nickel figures** (export values, quota volumes, China share) are BPS and ESDM figures carried by trade-press services (Petromindo, Mysteel, S&P Global, Databoks). Values match across carriers; re-read from BPS trade tables before publication.
- **F5**: the 60.5 % PPP share is from a commentary citing the IMF, and the 42 % market-rate share from a secondary tabulation of the April 2026 WEO; neither was read from the WEO database.
- **Technology access** (part of F12) was not evidenced; it belongs with cluster E.
- **Not done:** WTO country profile for Indonesia's share of world exports (O-6); Kemenperin's own manufacturing-share series; BPS "Statistik Industri Besar dan Sedang".
