How it works

Each review reads the report on its own terms, then checks it

  1. Read the report

    We log what the report assumed, the goals it set, and what it recommended, each with its page reference. Its authors' intent comes first.

  2. Check it against the evidence

    Each claim is checked against official statistics, with a date and a source. AI does the first reading; a named person checks each stage before anything is published.

  3. Publish, with a right of reply

    Every page shows what was covered, what was not, and every source. The report's authors are invited to respond on the page.
