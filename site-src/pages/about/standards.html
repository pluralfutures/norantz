About · Editorial standards

Read generously. Check rigorously.

The rules every review follows, whichever country and whichever government wrote the report.

Intent before change

Every finding starts with what the authors set out to do, then what the report said, then what the evidence shows now, then what follows. A report is read on its own terms before it is checked.

Describe, do not grade

Reviews say "below the path", "the report does not discuss", or "worth revisiting". They do not say a report failed or was wrong. There is no overall score, no percentage, and no ranking of countries.

Evidence

Readings rest on official statistics only. News reports never support a reading. Every figure has a date and a link. Figures read off a chart are marked as approximate and never rated.

AI, disclosed

Every page says which steps the AI did and which a person checked, by stage.
