Quoting a review fairly

  • Give the "reviewed as of" date. A review is a reading at that date.
  • Quote counts with their coverage: "11 of 38 items reviewed so far hold", not "11 hold".
  • Do not turn counts into a score, a percentage, or a ranking. Norantz does not grade governments or reports.
  • Link to the fixed version you quote, so readers see what you saw.
