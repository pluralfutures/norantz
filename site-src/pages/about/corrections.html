About · Corrections and changelog

Every change is dated, explained, and kept

Reviews are drafted with generative AI and will sometimes be wrong. When they are, we correct them in the open. This page sets out how, and logs every change.

Five kinds of change

KindWhen we use itVersion
CorrectionA factual error: a wrong figure, quote, date, page reference, or link.v1.1, v1.2…
ClarificationWording that could mislead, with no change to any figure or reading.v1.1, v1.2…
UpdateNew data or a new change since publication; readings may change. Usually on the review's planned date.v2, v3…
ReplyA response from the report's authors added to the page.No new version
WithdrawalA review, or part of one, that cannot stand. The page stays up with a notice saying why.New version

Any correction that changes a reading (for example, from "holds" to "holds in part") is treated as an update and gets a new whole-number version, so a citation to the earlier version stays accurate.

[TRIAL] Proposed version scheme for decision S10. Fixed addresses take the form /reports/{report}/v2/; minor versions replace the page at the same address with a dated note.

How a change appears on the page

  • A dated note sits at the top of the section that changed, saying what was there before and what is there now.
  • The review's header shows the current version and the date of the last change.
  • "Sources, versions and reuse", the last section of every review, lists every version with a link.
  • This page lists every change across the site, newest first.

We never change a published review without a note, except to fix spelling, broken formatting, or a broken link that does not change what a source says.

Reporting an error

Tell us the review, the section, what is wrong, and, if you can, the source that shows it. Use the error form. We acknowledge within 5 working days and correct confirmed factual errors within 10. If you wrote or published the report, see for report authors.
