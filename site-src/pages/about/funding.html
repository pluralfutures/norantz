Which reports are listed

The catalogue aims to list every national report that sets out a long-horizon future for a country: visions with an end state ten or more years out, and scenario sets. Ordinary medium-term plans are not listed.

[PLACEHOLDER] Wording follows the proposed default for method decision 15 (scope), not yet agreed.

Which listed reports are reviewed first

Reports are chosen for review by how much they still shape policy, how far into their horizon they are, and whether there is enough official data to test them.
