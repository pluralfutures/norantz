About · Right of reply

If you wrote or published a reviewed report

Every review page has a place for the authors' response. We aim for a fair reading, and we want to know where we have not given one.

Before publication

The publisher of the report is sent the draft review and invited to comment before it goes live.

After publication

You can send a response at any time. It appears in full in the "Response from the authors" section of the page, dated, and unedited apart from length. Where your response shows a factual error, we correct it and log the correction.
