Accessibility

Norantz should be readable by anyone, on any device, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, or zoom. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

Version 0.1 · 18 Nov 2026 · Draft for review

1. How far the site meets the standard

Partly conformant. The site has been built to WCAG 2.1 AA but has not yet been audited. Known gaps are listed below.

2. What we do

  • Every text colour meets AA contrast on its background, in both the light and dark themes. The site follows your device's dark-mode setting.
  • Status is never shown by colour alone: every reading has a shape and a word.
  • Every chart has a written reading beside it, and its figures are in a table.
  • The map has a full list of the same reports, which is the main view on a phone.
  • Pages are built to reflow on small screens and at high zoom without sideways scrolling.
  • Every page has a "skip to content" link, headings in order, and labelled form fields.
  • Reading text is set large (18 pixels) with generous line spacing.
  • Writing is plain: short sentences, one idea per paragraph, and a one-line summary at the top of every review.
  • Animation is kept to a minimum and stops if your device asks for reduced motion.

3. Known limits

  • The original reports we link to are published by others, often as long PDF files that may not be accessible.
  • Spanish and Indonesian pages are not yet translated, and some interface text is a machine translation.
  • Some wide tables scroll sideways inside their box on a phone.
  • Status shapes are small drawings; their word is always beside them, but the drawing itself has no separate description.

4. Tell us about a barrier

If something on the site is hard to use, or you need a review in another format, contact us and tell us the page and the problem. We reply within 5 working days and aim to fix the problem, or provide the content another way, within 20.

5. Legal basis

Spain's accessibility rules for public-sector websites (Real Decreto 1112/2018) do not apply to Norantz, which is run by a private company. Whether Ley 11/2023, which brings the European Accessibility Act into Spanish law, applies to a site of this kind is being checked. We follow WCAG 2.1 AA in either case.
