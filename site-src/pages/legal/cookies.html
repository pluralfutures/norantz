1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels. Spanish law requires your informed consent before a site stores or reads anything on your device, unless it is strictly needed for a service you asked for.

The site does not use advertising, social media buttons, or tracking. The "show draft notes" switch on draft pages remembers nothing once you leave.

3. Fonts and other outside requests

The site currently loads its fonts from Google Fonts. This sets no cookies, but your browser sends your IP address to Google to fetch them. Before launch the fonts will be hosted on our own server. See the privacy policy.

4. Measuring use of the site

If we measure how many people read each review, we will use a tool that sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and does not identify you. That needs no consent banner. If that ever changes, we will ask for consent first and update this page.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Doing so will not affect how this site works.
