Privacy

3. Generative AI and your data

Norantz uses generative AI to read published reports and official statistics. It does not use AI to make decisions about you, and does not profile visitors. We do not use your messages or email address to train AI models.

If we use an AI tool to help sort or draft answers to messages, we use a provider that does not keep or train on the content, under a data processing agreement, and a person reads and sends every answer.

5. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • see the data we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • restrict how we use it, or object to our use of it;
  • send it to you or another organisation in a common format;
  • withdraw your consent, for example by unsubscribing. This does not affect what we did before.

Write to [privacy email] and say which right you want to use. We answer within one month. We may ask you to confirm who you are.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD), at www.aepd.es, or to the authority in the EU country where you live.

6. Other points

Children
The site is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly accept sign-ups from anyone under 14, the age of consent in Spain.
Security
We keep data on providers that encrypt it in transit and at rest, and limit access to the people who need it.
Automated decisions
None are made about you.
Changes
If we change this policy, we change the date at the top and list the change in the changelog. If a change affects how we use your email address, we tell subscribers first.
