2. What this site is

Norantz publishes reviews of national vision and scenario reports, setting what each report assumed and aimed for beside official evidence published since. The reviews are drafted with generative AI, checked by people, and may contain errors. They are offered as a starting point for public discussion and for deeper, human-led analysis. They are not professional, financial, legal, or policy advice.
