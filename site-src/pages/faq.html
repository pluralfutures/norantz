Frequently asked questions

Is this a ranking?

No. Norantz does not score, grade, or rank countries, governments, or reports. Each page counts its readings by label, always beside how much of the report has been reviewed. What each label means.

Who writes the reviews?

AI does the reading and the first draft, in the same steps for every report. A named person checks each stage before anything is published. The nine steps.

Why use AI?

So every report can be read the same way, at scale, and read again when new data arrives. Each page discloses what the AI did and what people checked.

Why this report and not another?

The catalogue lists every report in scope; reviews follow published criteria. How reports are chosen. You can suggest one.

Can the authors reply?

Yes, on the page itself. For report authors.

How often are reviews updated?

Each review shows the date of its next planned update. Every update gets a new fixed version, and the old one stays online.

How do I cite a review?

Cite the fixed version address. Data and citation.

How do I report an error?

Use the error form. Corrections are dated and logged.
