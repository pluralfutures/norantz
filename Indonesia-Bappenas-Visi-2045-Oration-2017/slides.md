## Slide 7 — Demografi Indonesia
*Indonesian demography* · type: chart · section: megatrends_demography

**Summary (EN):** Indonesia's population is projected to grow from 238.5 m (2010) to 318.7 m (2045) while TFR falls from 2.5 to 1.9, life expectancy rises from 69.8 to 72.8 years, the 65+ population grows from 11.9 m to 42.8 m, and urbanisation rises from 49.9 % to 69.1 %. The dependency ratio bottoms out at 46.9 % in 2028–2031 — the demographic-bonus window — before climbing back to 50.1 % by 2045. Indonesia holds 41 % of ASEAN's productive-age population. Seven expected conditions include human-centred development, use of the first and second demographic bonus, urbanisation control and comprehensive social protection.

## Slide 8 — Sebagian besar penduduk masih tinggal di Jawa
*Most of the population still lives in Java* · type: text_with_map · section: megatrends_demography

**Summary (EN):** Java-Bali holds the bulk of Indonesia's population (140.9 m in 2010, 176.2 m in 2045, density rising from 1,042 to 1,303/km²) though its share slips from 59 % to 55 %; Sumatra rises to 23 %, Kalimantan to 7 %, Sulawesi 8 %, while Maluku-Papua stays at 3 % (10.3 m, 21/km²). Urban share grows from 49.9 % to 69.1 %; by 2035 nearly 90 % of Java's population is urban, with a Jakarta–Bandung mega-urban region of 76 million. ## Slide 9 — Pertumbuhan Ekonomi 2016 - 2045: Menuju Negara Pendapatan Tinggi dan Salah Satu PDB Terbesar Dunia
*Economic growth 2016–2045: towards a high-income country and one of the world's largest economies* · type: table · section: economic_scenario

**Summary (EN):** Two 2016–2045 scenarios against the 1986–2015 record (5.1 % growth, GDP rank 16, USD 3,378 per capita). Baseline/BAU: 5.1 % growth, rank 8, USD 19,794 per capita by 2045, investment 33.1 % of GDP, industry 22.5 %. High scenario (structural reform on track, strong world economy): 6.4 % average growth, rank 4, USD 28,934 per capita, investment growth 7.3 %/yr reaching 39.0 % of GDP, exports 7.9 %/yr with openness 54 %, industry 7.8 %/yr reaching 32.0 % of GDP, agriculture 3.0 %/yr falling to 6.5 % of GDP.

**Text (ID, verbatim):**
