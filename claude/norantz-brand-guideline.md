# Norantz brand guideline: summary

Version 0.1, 17 November 2026. Draft for review. Full guideline (design system): https://claude.ai/artifact/DRZLvr6GwRJSaThHXB2pe5

## Decisions taken with the user
- Norantz is an **endorsed sub-brand**: "Norantz, by Plural Futures".
- Built on the **Plural Futures v2 (Remix)** system: Bricolage Grotesque, Newsreader, JetBrains Mono, warm paper, five futures-inks.
- Status is shown with **neutral colour and shape**: no traffic lights.
- The name: Basque for "towards where?" (nora + -ntz). Kept as a question.

## The core of the brand
- Brand idea: **the second reader**, a critical friend. Principle: **Read generously. Check rigorously.**
- Tagline: **Towards where?** Descriptor: **A second reading of national futures.**
- Writing: the consulting iceberg. One-line tip, 120-word summary above a visible **waterline**, action titles, evidence below. Every finding in four beats: intent, then, now, so what.
- Colour: **colour tells time, shape tells status.** Loam = what the report said; Sky (signature ink) = what the evidence shows. Ember only for one call to action. Other inks for scenarios only. Spectrum for covers, the home hero and scenario ranges.
- Dotted = expected or prospective; solid = observed.
- Marks: the **bearing** (dotted planned heading, solid actual heading from one origin, in a Sky tile); a drawn monoline wordmark whose z runs on in Sky.
- Status families: bearing markers (look back), target position, field chips (look ahead, dashed), recommendation chips.
