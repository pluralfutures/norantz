# UU 59/2024 — enacted RPJPN 2025-2045 annex, reconciled against the final draft

17 November 2026. Closes the "obtain the enacted annex" step in `claude/indonesia-2045-review-scope.md` §9.

## Source

- **Document:** Undang-Undang Nomor 59 Tahun 2024 tentang RPJPN 2025-2045, Salinan (official copy), 371 PDF pages: law body pp. 1-17, Penjelasan pp. 18-31, **Lampiran (annex) pp. 32-371 = printed pp. i-334**. Promulgated 13 September 2024, Lembaran Negara 2024 No. 194, TLN 6987.
- **Downloaded from:** JDIH Kemenko Infrastruktur, `https://jdih.kemenkoinfra.go.id/dokumen/file/uu/2024/uu-nomor-59-tahun-2024.pdf` (12.5 MB). Same salinan is mirrored on regional JDIH sites (Mimika, Subang). The BPK database lists only the 17-page law body; the Kemenko Infra copy carries the full 371-page LN version.
- **Format:** scanned salinan with an OCR text layer (noisy on tables; figures are images). Not watermarked — this is the public, citable text. Local working copy and page renders are in the session workspace (`uu59/`), not in the project.
- **Dating clue for the draft:** Gambar 5.2.1 in both versions is sourced "Exercise Bappenas (2023) per 22 Juni 2023", so the Rancangan Akhir post-dates 22 June 2023. Sources in Bab III-IV are "diolah Bappenas (2023)". The final draft is therefore H2 2023.

## Verdict

The enacted annex is the final draft with light edits. Structure (6 Bab, 5 targets, 8 missions, 17 directions, 45 indicators, 20 game changers, 4 phases), the growth path, the regional trajectory and 43 of the 45 indicator rows are unchanged. **The draft can be scored against with a short list of overrides**, below. Page numbers match within one page (draft annex 335 pp., enacted 334 pp.).

### Five headline targets (Gambar 3.4.1, printed p. 66)

All nine draft rows identical (GNI pc 5,500-5,520 → 30,300; maritime 8.1 → 15.0 %; manufacturing 20.8 → 28.0 %; poverty 7.0-8.0 → 0.5-0.8 %; Gini 0.379-0.382 → 0.290-0.320; KTI 21.4 → 28.5 %; GPI 34 → top 15; HCI 0.56 → 0.73; GHG intensity reduction 38.6 → 93.5 %). **One row added** under target 5:

| Indicator | 2025 baseline | 2045 target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Indeks Kualitas Lingkungan Hidup (IKLH) | 76.49 | 83.00 |

The text of 3.4 (both versions) gives the income target as a **range, USD 23,000-30,300**, while the figure prints 30,300 only. Record both; the range is the authors' own lower bound.

## What this means for the review

1. **Score against the draft extraction, with four overrides:** add IKLH 76.49 → 83.00 (target 5); add formal-job creation 35 → 80 (IE4); replace IKLH row 42a with the four sub-indices; treat the vision as "Bersatu, Berdaulat, Maju, Berkelanjutan". Everything else in the project's `part1/part2` extractions can be cited as the enacted figure.
2. **Citation:** the enacted salinan carries no "Tidak untuk Dikutip" mark. Cite as "UU No. 59 Tahun 2024, Lampiran, printed p. N". This resolves method proposal 23 for this report: quote the law, not the draft.
3. **New register items:** formal-job share 35 → 80 % is a goal (IE4); the four environmental sub-indices are goals (IE15). The 36.65 / 32.65 discrepancy is an N3 inconsistency.
4. **Standing (N1):** in force as law since 13 Sept 2024; first five-year plan under it is Perpres 12/2025 (RPJMN 2025-2029), which is change-scan candidate X1 and still to be obtained.
5. **Not re-checked line by line:** the narrative text of Bab I-II, V and VI, and the 29 × 34 provincial baseline tables (Tabel 5.1.x). Spot-checks found no differences; a full text diff can be run from the OCR layer if a rated figure comes from those parts.
