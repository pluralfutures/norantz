# Indonesia Emas 2045 (RPJPN 2025-2045) — review scope

Draft brief, 17 November 2026. Scopes a focused run of method 0.2 on the Bappenas RPJPN 2025-2045 final-draft annex, using the Indonesia project folders. For human confirmation before the evidence pass.

## 1. The decision

Run a **claims-and-change-scan review**, not the full normative track.

Reason: the vision was enacted in September 2024, so under two years have elapsed. Goals (45 headline indicators, 2025 → 2045) would almost all rate "too early", and commitments have barely had a budget cycle. What *can* be tested now is whether the assumptions the plan rests on, and the issues it names as the problems to solve, still hold given developments since 2023-24.

This is not a track the method defines. It is closest to the reduced review for superseded visions (claims and enabling assumptions tested, goals shown but not rated), applied for a different reason: the vision is in force but early. Proposed as **decision 21** for `scenario-review-method.md`: an "early-horizon variant" for in-force visions with fewer than ~5 years elapsed.

### Absent-topic check (to run in N2)

Topics to look for and record as "absent" or "mentioned in passing": generative AI as a distinct force (the plan has "disruption" and a 40 % jobs figure, Gambar 2.1.2); tariff or trade-war scenarios; a change of government or policy discontinuity; a pandemic or health shock after COVID-19; a dedicated sovereign wealth or investment vehicle; universal programmes with large recurrent cost; financial-sector stress; critical-mineral export restrictions by other countries.

## 7. Limits to log from the start

- Source is the draft annex, not the enacted law. Every figure marked "draft".
- Both sources carry a non-citation watermark.
- Two years elapsed: most ratings will be "no evidence" or "watch"; this is expected and should be said on the page.
- Baseline 2025 in the indicator tables is a projection, not an outturn.
- Figures read from charts rather than printed are marked "~" in the extraction and must not be rated.
- OCR residuals in italic English terms; cross-check any exact figure against the page image before it is rated.
- Keynote figures are the May 2023 version and may differ from the annex; use the annex where both exist.
