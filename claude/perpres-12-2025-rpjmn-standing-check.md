## Standing-check finding (N1)

The vision is in force and its first five-year plan was issued within the statutory window. The Ringkasan opens by quoting the enacted vision ("NKRI yang Bersatu, Berdaulat, Maju, dan Berkelanjutan", UU 59/2024) and the five 2045 targets, and states that the RPJMN is Tahap I of the RPJPN, "berfokus pada perkuatan fondasi transformasi" (p. 2). The President's programme is merged in as **Asta Cita** (8 national priorities), 17 Program Prioritas, 8 Program Hasil Terbaik Cepat, 81 Kegiatan Prioritas Utama and a PSN list set through the annual RKP.

**Core result.** The RPJMN keeps the RPJPN's 2025 baselines and its 2029 structural end-points (manufacturing 21.9 % of GDP; 2025 figures copied from Tabel 4.x.x) but sets a **growth path above the vision's phase-1 range**: 5.3 % (2025), 6.3 %, 7.5 %, 7.7 %, **8.0 % (2029)** (p. 14), against the RPJPN's 5.6-6.1 % for 2025-2029. Continuity assumption F10 is supported; growth assumption F1 is now carried by a steeper medium-term path than the vision itself assumed.

## Programmes, projects, financing (register candidates)

- **Asta Cita / 8 PN:** 1 ideology, democracy, human rights, defence; 2 food-energy-water self-sufficiency, green/blue economy; 3 infrastructure, jobs, cooperatives, creative and agro-maritime industry; 4 human capital, science, health, sport, gender, youth, disability; 5 downstreaming and industrialisation; 6 build from villages, poverty eradication; 7 political, legal and bureaucratic reform, revenue; 8 environment, culture, tolerance.
- **Quick wins (Program Hasil Terbaik Cepat):** Makan Bergizi Gratis for students, santri, pregnant and breastfeeding mothers and under-fives (KPU 46-47); free health checks (44); TB elimination (45); complete quality hospitals in every kabupaten/kota (50); leprosy and schistosomiasis elimination (48); sekolah unggul; school renovation.
- **Among the 81 Kegiatan Prioritas Utama:** Lumbung Pangan / KSPP food estates in Kalimantan Tengah, Sumatra, Papua and elsewhere; five priority tourism destinations; electricity grid and digital ecosystem; salt and marine-product industry; production cooperatives; early-childhood education; mother-and-child mortality; stunting.
- **Danantara:** listed in PN 5's regulatory framework as a proposed law establishing a sui generis body to manage investment and SOEs (p. 40). So it *is* in the RPJMN, as an intended institution.
- **Investment need 2025-2029:** Rp 47,573.45 tn, average Rp 9,514.69 tn/yr: government Rp 3,435.04 tn (7.22 %), SOEs Rp 2,915.14 tn (6.13 %), private/households Rp 41,223.26 tn (86.65 %) (p. 22). Financing shares: government 7.22; SOE internal funds 4.04; bank credit 16.91; equity 1.35; corporate bonds 2.46; household/corporate internal funds 68.01 (p. 23). Financial-sector contribution targeted at 20.73 %/yr.
- **Regional themes (Bab 3):** Sumatra "mata rantai utama bioindustri"; Java "megalopolis unggul, inovatif, inklusif"; Bali-Nusra tourism and creative-economy superhub; Kalimantan "Superhub Ekonomi Nusantara"; Sulawesi its supporting hub; Maluku maritime hub; Papua "sehat, cerdas, produktif". Same corridor themes as RPJPN Tabel 4.2.1. Regional 2029 growth (LI, p. 94-95): KTI 8.8 %, KBI 7.7 %; Maluku 11.1, Sulawesi 9.9, Bali-NT 8.4, Kalimantan 8.2, Java 7.9, Papua 7.7, Sumatra 7.2.

## Readings for the register

| Item | Reading | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F10 continuity | **Supported** | RPJMN issued on time, quotes the enacted vision, copies baselines and the phase-1 manufacturing end-point. |
| F1 / 3.1 growth path | **Under pressure, from above** | Operative path 5.3 → 8.0 % by 2029, mean ≈ 7 %, vs the vision's 5.6-6.1 %. The required-rate test should run both paths; the 8 % path makes the 2045 arithmetic easier if met and the 2029 shortfall larger if not. |
| F9 fiscal space | **Watch** | Tax ratio 2025 (10.24 %) is below the RPJPN's own 2025 baseline (10.70-11.20); 2029 range 11.52-15.00 reaches the vision's 2045 line only at its top. MBG, free health checks, housing and food estates are new recurrent/capital lines not in the RPJPN. |
| 6.1 non-APBN financing | **Supported in intent** | 86.65 % of the Rp 47.6 tn need assigned to private/household sources; 68 % to internal funds. |
| F8 / 4.1 energy and GHG | **Under pressure** | Renewables 23 % by 2029 against 70 % by 2045; GHG-reduction series on a different basis (18.37 vs 28.12 for 2025). Needs a definitional note before rating; on its face the energy path has moved away from the vision's. |
| F6 / 3.3 middle class | **Definitional gap** | RPJMN middle class 17.13 → 20 % (2029); RPJPN 38 → 80 %. Different cut-offs. Record both and choose one series for the scorecard. |
| C1.4, 3 others | Baselines revised | TB 272 → 329 (up); employment social security 43.19 → 32.15 (down); disabled formal work 22 → 10 (down); PISA 2025 "baseline" 396/404/416 kept although the 2022 outturn is 359/366/383. |

## Absent-topic check additions

Present in the RPJMN, absent in the RPJPN: Makan Bergizi Gratis; free health checks; sekolah unggul; food estates (Lumbung Pangan); 3-million-houses (LI p. 187: 1.7 m new, 5.3 m upgraded, 2 m PSU); Danantara (as a proposed law); a state revenue agency (LI); Islamic-economy and blue-economy indices; Koperasi Desa Merah Putih is **not** in the Ringkasan (it post-dates the Perpres; treat as change-scan candidate X5). Still absent in both: generative AI as a distinct force, tariff-war scenarios, pandemic recurrence.

## Limits

- The Ringkasan is Bappenas's summary, not the Perpres annex; where the two could differ, Lampiran I-II govern. Lampiran II (year-by-year matrices) was not read.
- Lampiran I figures marked (LI) come from a noisy OCR extraction and must be checked against the page image.
- Three 2025 baselines in the Ringkasan are projections set in late 2024; 2025 outturns are now available and should be used in the evidence pass instead.
